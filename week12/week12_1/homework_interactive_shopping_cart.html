Homework: Interactive Shopping Cart

The homework is built up in five sections. Each section adds one requirement, and Section 5 combines them into the finished cart.

  • 1 · Product list
  • 2 · Add / Remove buttons
  • 3 · Cart sidebar
  • 4 · Quantity controls
  • 5 · ShoppingCart component

Section 1 · Product list

Requirement: at least 5 items, each with a name, price and image URL.

    Section 2 · Add / Remove buttons

    Requirement: add and remove buttons. State is an array of cart items.

      
      
      

      
      
        
      Section 3 · Cart sidebar
      
        
      Requirement: a sidebar showing the cart items and the total price, computed from state.
      
        
      
          
        
          
        
        
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Section 4 · Quantity controls
        
        
        Requirement: + and − buttons for each item in the cart.
        
        
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Section 5 · ShoppingCart component
        
        
        Requirement: a ShoppingCart component made of the product list and the cart panel. This is the finished cart, combining Sections 1–4.
